01 — THE PEOPLE & THE STORY
ROOTED IN INDORE

A family food journey

Built on flavour.
Carried forward with pride.

AV Madhuram is associated with the Girothiya family and the brothers Aayush and Vaibhav Girothiya. The brand's story is closely connected with Indore's love for generous, flavour-packed street food.

From the signature sandwich to the energy around its outlets, the next chapter is about bringing the Madhuram experience to more people and more cities.
